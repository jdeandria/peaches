# Product decisions

Resolved design questions, so the reasoning survives the conversation
that produced it. Change a decision here first, then the code.

| # | Question | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | One workout per matchup or per round? | **Per matchup.** Both Peaches in a matchup do the identical session, drawn from exercises they can both do. The round's focus keeps different matchups comparable. | A per-round session would shrink to the intersection of the whole Crate's capabilities, which gets thin fast. Fairness matters most between the two people being compared. |
| 2 | Are capabilities per Crate or per Peach? | **Per Peach, global.** | "I can do pull-ups" is a property of the person. One list to maintain. |
| 3 | Handicap between fitness levels? | **No handicap.** Same prescription (capped at the weaker Peach's level + 1), scored on execution relative to the prescription. | Simple, legible, and already favours completion over raw strength. Revisit if standings show level dominating. |
| 4 | Capability vs. equipment on the day? | **Deferred.** Capabilities describe what a Peach can do with gear they normally have. | Per-day "where am I training" is real but adds a prompt before every session. Roadmap item. |
| 5 | When does a round close? | **Automatically**: end of the exercise day in the Crate's time zone plus `loggingGraceHours` (default 6). Submissions after that are refused. Owners can still close early. Any member's client runs the sync on launch and refresh. | Deterministic and the same for everyone in a Crate wherever they live. |
| 12 | Server component? | **None.** Client + Firestore only. Round opening and closing are idempotent (seeded, deterministic ids) so any device can do them and they converge. | Simplicity and cost. The honour system already accepts client trust. |
| 6 | Verifying logs? | **Honour system.** | It's a game among friends. Photo evidence or peer confirmation is a roadmap item if cheating becomes a problem. |
| 7 | Two Crates with the same exercise day? | **Two workouts.** One log cannot count for two Crates. | Each Crate's workout is tailored to its matchup; merging would break fairness. The generator already keeps sessions short. |
| 8 | Joining mid-season? | **Wait for the next Season.** Participants freeze when a Season is scheduled. | Round-robin schedules do not take late entrants without unfairness. |
| 9 | Byes? | Points-for recorded, no win or loss. Standings rank by win percentage (ties count half), then point differential, then points for. | Standard fantasy convention. |
| 10 | App identity | The PWA manifest `id` is the site root, so an installed copy is identified by its origin (`https://<host>/`). | A PWA has no package id; moving hosts means users reinstall, so pick the production domain before launch. |
| 11 | Auth and backend | **Firebase Auth** (Google, Apple, email) and **Firestore**, behind the existing repository interfaces. | Matches the Firebase Hosting choice; one console for web, Android and iOS. |
| 13 | Accent colour from the peach seed | **M3 *fruit salad* variant** of #FFE5B4 for accents (coral-peach primary, the seed's gold as tertiary); a hue-free neutral scheme for every surface role. | The seed's own tone-40 accent reads gold-brown, not peach; fruit salad keeps the same seed but lands on peach-fruit coral, matching the 🍑 icon. Neutral surfaces keep backgrounds clean grey and white. |
| 14 | Material 3 Expressive | **Adopt it, implemented in-house** in `src/app/ui/expressive/` on top of Angular Material's M3 theme: spring motion tokens, wavy progress, the shape library for avatars, a morphing loading indicator, press-morphing buttons, connected button groups, emphasized type. | Angular Material has no Expressive components yet. Owning a few small components keeps the look stable and makes swapping to official ones a contained change when they ship. |
| 15 | Weight logging | Every set of an exercise that **takes a load** (any free weight, machine, sled, sandbag or plate, or a loaded measurement) records its weight. Stored in **kg**, shown and stepped in the Peach's units (2.5 kg / 5 lb). Pre-filled from the last time that exercise was logged; editing a set's weight carries forward to later sets that held the same weight. | Weight never affects scoring, which stays relative to the prescription, so Peaches with different gear and strength still compete fairly. It feeds progress (volume per workout) and makes the next session's pre-fill useful. |
| 16 | Platform | **An Angular progressive web app.** One codebase served from Firebase Hosting, installable from the browser on Android, iOS and desktop, offline-capable via the Angular service worker and Firestore's persistent cache. No app-store builds. | One build and one deployment for every platform, no store review cycle, and updates reach installed copies on their next launch. |
| 17 | Time zones | New Crates and Peaches take the creator's device time zone rather than UTC. The demo is seeded in the visitor's zone so "today" is their today. | Round windows are defined in the Crate's zone (#5); UTC put evening exercise days on the wrong date for most of the world. |
